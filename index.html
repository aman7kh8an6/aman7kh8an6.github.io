Hey, I am Aman

data engineering, databases, and machine learning for biology. always building.

I am a research intern at Prof. Schliep's medical bioinformatics lab and an MSc Artificial Intelligence student at BTU Cottbus-Senftenberg. At the lab I work on retrieving binding sites for oligonucleotide drugs fast enough to search a very large sequence database, using learned embeddings and nearest-neighbour search instead of brute-force thermodynamics.

Before that I spent almost four years as a data engineer at Infosys on Apple's data platform, where I owned 300+ production ETL pipelines moving multi-million-row tables out of SAP HANA into Oracle and Snowflake. Somewhere in there I became more interested in what the database was doing underneath than in what I was asking it to do.

So I am writing a storage engine in C++ one layer at a time, I implement things like PCA from first principles rather than importing them, and I read papers on indexing and retrieval next to papers on DNA. I learn systems by rebuilding them.
