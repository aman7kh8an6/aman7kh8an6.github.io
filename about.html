About

data engineer turned AI researcher, based in Berlin.

The short version

I am Aman Khan. I spent almost four years as a data engineer at Infosys, working on Apple's data platform, where I owned 300+ production pipelines moving multi-million-row tables from SAP HANA into Oracle and Snowflake. That work made me curious about what was happening underneath the systems I used every day, which is how I ended up in an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at BTU Cottbus-Senftenberg, and then in a bioinformatics lab.

The way I learn is to rebuild things. I wrote a storage engine in C++ to understand B+Trees, implemented PCA with orthogonal iteration instead of calling a library, and built an information retrieval system with its own stemming and Boolean search. It is slower than reading about them, and it sticks.

What I am looking for

Working student and internship roles in data engineering, ML engineering or research engineering, anywhere in Germany or remote. I am also hoping to continue in the lab toward a master's thesis.
